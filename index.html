TargetX Alert Notes v1.1

A Canvas bookmarklet that summarizes each student's grade, missing work, and last activity, then copies a note you can paste into TargetX.

TargetX Alert Notes v1.1
  1. Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar. If you have an older version, delete that bookmark.
  2. Open any Canvas course where you're a teacher, TA, or admin.
  3. Click the bookmark. Adjust thresholds, then use Copy note on a student and paste it into the TargetX comment or alert.
